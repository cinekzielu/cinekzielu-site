The 26–28 minute films and varied single-day footage support 20 frames per trip. Frames come from original 3840×2160 DJI recordings, exported as 2560, 960 and 480 px WebP. No generated content, upscaling, color changes or source-file modifications. Source paths, timestamps, native PNGs and selection evidence remain in private local records.

Both trips connect to their verified film, existing summit, Slovakia/Tatry and 2025 filters, search and sharing metadata. Huncowski also links to the Kieżmarski materials.

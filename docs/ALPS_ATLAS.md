# Across the Alps in the shared atlas

Local package, 11 October 2026. Publication requires approval.

The five August 2026 visits confirmed in the author's post-trip account now
appear in the shared Alps atlas and the relevant country: Triglav / Slovenia,
Grossglockner / Austria, Dufourspitze / Switzerland, Vorder Grauspitz /
Liechtenstein, and Zugspitze / Germany. Existing IDs and geometry stay intact.

`alpsPlaces.js` derives positions, activity statistics and story chapter links
from `alpsStory.js`, using the same verified OSM summit nodes. No GPS tracks,
summit dates or border crossings have been inferred. Dufourspitze statistics
cover the full three-day stage, including approach and return.

Each summit has its 2026 activity card and a direct chapter link. The 48 images
are explicitly labelled as the whole-trip gallery. The existing trip teaser
appears at country/area level; no summit episode is represented as released.

Alps and Switzerland preserve the 2025/2026 year filter and area context.
The five points belong only to 2026. Country membership feeds trip/gallery
filters and search without duplicating the expedition or its photos.

Validation: build, existing checks plus `scripts/check-alps-atlas.mjs`;
browser proof and any limitations are recorded in the private project state.
